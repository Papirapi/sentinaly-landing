Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sentinaly?

Sentinaly is the world's first Agentic Regulatory Intelligence OS built for health and life science industry leaders. It continuously monitors regulatory and policy activity, analyses what matters to your organization and forecasts what is coming — turning regulatory complexity into strategic foresight and competitive leverage.

Who is Sentinaly for?

Regulatory affairs, public and government affairs, policy, scientific and innovation leaders in pharmaceutical, medtech, biotech and health organizations — from startups to large multinationals, as well as industry associations and advisory firms.

What does "agentic" mean here?

Rather than a static database you have to search, Sentinaly's modules act on your behalf: they track sources, detect changes, assess impact and surface recommendations proactively — so your team anticipates regulatory shifts weeks in advance instead of reacting to them.

Which languages and jurisdictions are covered?

The platform works natively in English and French. Coverage spans major health and life science regulators and policy bodies globally; specific jurisdictions and sources are scoped with you during onboarding.

How is my data protected?

Data is encrypted end to end, isolated per customer and governed by user-controlled, role-based access. Our infrastructure is ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type I and II compliant. Read more on our Data Protection page.

Does Sentinaly replace legal or regulatory counsel?

No. Sentinaly gives your team the intelligence to decide faster and with more confidence, but it is not legal advice. It is designed to complement your regulatory and legal experts, not replace them.

How do I get started?

Book a demo and we'll walk you through the platform, scope your regulatory priorities and set up a tailored workspace for your team. You can also reach us at contact@sentinaly.com.
